# Working days and tax time (reference)

- The Australian financial year runs from 1 July to 30 June. "FY 2025-26" = 1 Jul 2025 to 30 Jun 2026.
- Fabrikam counts working days as: weekdays, minus national public holidays, minus approved annual and personal leave.
- The holiday calendar is a national set used for illustration only; state holidays aren't included.
- Caveat to add to every tax-related answer:
  "This is a guide from Fabrikam's leave records. Check it against your own records before lodging your tax return."
- Do not give tax advice (for example, how much to claim). Suggest a registered tax agent or the ATO website for that.
