# Fabrikam leave policy (fictional, for the workshop)

- Annual leave: 20 days a year, accrued from the start of each financial year. Unused leave carries over (opening balance).
- Personal (sick or carer's) leave: 10 days a year. It can be requested the same day or in arrears.
- Annual leave can't start in the past. Please give at least 2 weeks' notice for 5 or more days.
- Requests are counted in working days. Weekends and national public holidays aren't deducted.
- Every request goes to the employee's direct manager. Managers' own leave goes to HR.
- Requests can be deleted while they're pending, or after approval if the leave hasn't started.
